**What happened**

> THE FIRST HALF IS THE BEAT AND THE SECOND HALF IS WASTE. Trim 0.00 to 4.30: the macro on the dented enamel bowl, the continuous rise, and the street revealed with the seated rows and the crows. Everything wrong happens after it.
> 
> THE DESIGN FAULT, AND IT IS THE REAL LESSON. The prompt asked for a specific person, 'a straight-backed middle-aged man', at a distance, with no reference image and no face. He came back BALD, because nothing described his hair and there was nothing to copy it from. The decision not to attach @dr_roy_middle was deliberate, to avoid asking identity of a face that would be a few dozen pixels, but the consequence was not thought through: a man who cannot be identified is not Roy, he is a stranger. **If a beat does not need a particular person, put nobody in it.** The episode loses nothing, because beat 2 puts Roy in the courtyard four seconds later.
> 
> TWO CONSEQUENCES OF THE SAME HALF-COMMITMENT. The four younger men were written 'far down the centre of the street, small in the frame' and arrived in the extreme foreground with the backs of their heads filling the bottom of the frame from about 5.4, which is the frame edge failing again. And the man approaches before walking away, which Chand saw as 'running to the center and suddenly turning'. That is the THIRD time a move named by its direction has gone the wrong way in this project, after the flipped feet in ep01 beat 1 and the stairs in ep03 beat 3. The remedy the skill records, naming what the move ends on, was applied here, 'moving steadily toward the pale haze at the far end of the street', and it did not prevent it. **Record that the remedy has now been tested once and did not hold.**
> 
> WHAT WORKED, AND IT IS MOST OF THE SHOT. The bowl macro is exactly the hook it was meant to be: dented enamel on grimy stone with straw and a torn scrap of cloth. The continuous rise reads smoothly. And the ravaged street landed: cracked plaster, dark water stains, worn greyed cloth on the seated people with their belongings around them, crows on the roadway. Chand's question before the run was whether it would look too clean and polished, and naming the dirt as objects rather than touching the grade clause is what stopped it.
> 
> DIGNITY HELD AND NOTHING WAS REFUSED. No bodies, no visible emaciation, heads bare rather than wrapped head to foot, which was corrected before the run after the same corpse reading was caught in beat 2's cots.
